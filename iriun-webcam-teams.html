Teams guide

Iriun Webcam in Microsoft Teams on Windows — setup & fix guide

Select Iriun Webcam in Microsoft Teams Device Settings on Windows. Fix camera not showing, black screen and reconnection issues in Teams.

Iriun Webcam works in Microsoft Teams as a virtual camera. Start the Iriun PC client before opening Teams and select Iriun Webcam in Teams Device Settings.
Setup

Use Iriun Webcam in Microsoft Teams

  • 1

    Start Iriun and connect phone

    Open the Iriun PC client on Windows. Connect your phone via USB or Wi-Fi. Confirm the phone preview is active.

  • 2

    Open Teams Settings

    In Teams: click your profile picture (top right) → Settings → Devices.

  • 3

    Select Iriun Webcam

    In the Camera section, open the dropdown and select Iriun Webcam. A preview appears below the dropdown.

  • 4

    Test in a call

    Join a test call or start a meeting. Click the camera toggle to turn on video — Teams uses Iriun Webcam automatically.

Common Teams issues

Fix Iriun not working in Teams

Iriun not showing in Teams camera dropdown
Teams opened before Iriun was connected
Close Teams fully, start Iriun + connect phone, reopen Teams
Teams shows black screen with Iriun
Windows 11 camera permission not granted to Teams
Settings > Privacy & security > Camera > enable for Teams
Iriun keeps disconnecting during Teams calls
Wi-Fi instability during the call
Switch to USB mode for stable calls
FAQ

Teams questions

Iriun webcam not connecting in Teams after update
Teams updates sometimes reset device settings. Go to Settings → Devices and reselect Iriun Webcam. Also check camera permissions: Settings → Privacy → Camera → make sure Microsoft Teams is allowed.
Can I use Iriun for Teams background blur?
Yes. Once Iriun Webcam is selected in Teams, all Teams video features work normally including background blur, background replacement and noise suppression — these are processed by Teams, not Iriun.
