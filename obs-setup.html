Iriun Webcam in OBS Studio on Windows — setup & fix guide

Add Iriun Webcam as a Video Capture Device in OBS Studio on Windows. USB vs Wi-Fi settings, resolution and FPS recommendations, and fix camera not showing in OBS.

Iriun Webcam appears as a standard virtual camera device in OBS. Add it as a Video Capture Device source — same as any physical webcam.
Setup

Add Iriun Webcam to OBS Studio

  • 1

    Start Iriun PC client and connect phone

    Open Iriun Webcam on Windows first. Connect your phone via USB or Wi-Fi. Confirm the phone is streaming before opening OBS.

  • 2

    Add Video Capture Device in OBS

    In OBS: click + in the Sources panel → Video Capture Device → give it a name (e.g. "Iriun Phone") → click OK.

  • 3

    Select Iriun Webcam

    In the Device dropdown, select Iriun Webcam. The preview should show your phone camera feed. Click OK.

  • 4

    Set resolution and FPS

    In the Video Capture Device properties: set Resolution to your target (720p or 1080p) and FPS to 30. Higher FPS requires USB connection for stable performance.

FAQ

OBS questions

Iriun microphone audio in OBS
Iriun can stream phone microphone audio to Windows. In OBS, add an Audio Input Capture source and select Iriun Webcam (or the Iriun audio device). This gives you phone microphone audio as a separate audio track in OBS. See audio guide.
Can I use Iriun as a phone camera in OBS for streaming?
Yes — this is a popular use case. Many streamers use a phone as a high-quality webcam via Iriun because phone cameras often have better sensors than dedicated webcams. USB mode ensures consistent quality during long streams.
