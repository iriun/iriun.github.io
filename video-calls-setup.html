Setup guide

Iriun Webcam for Zoom, Teams & Google Meet on Windows

Select Iriun Webcam in Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet on Windows. The PC client must be running before you open your video call app.

Iriun Webcam works as a standard virtual camera in Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. The PC client must be running and your phone connected before you open the video call app.
Zoom

Use Iriun Webcam in Zoom

  • 1

    Start Iriun and connect phone

    Open the Iriun PC client on Windows. Connect your phone via USB or Wi-Fi. Confirm the phone preview is streaming.

  • 2

    Open Zoom Settings

    In Zoom: click the gear icon (Settings) → Video tab.

  • 3

    Select Iriun Webcam

    In the Camera dropdown, select Iriun Webcam. The preview should show your phone camera feed.

  • 4

    Enable HD (optional)

    In Zoom Video settings: check Enable HD for higher quality. Requires a stable USB or 5GHz Wi-Fi connection.

Microsoft Teams

Use Iriun Webcam in Microsoft Teams

  • 1

    Start Iriun and connect phone

    Open the Iriun PC client and connect your phone before opening Teams.

  • 2

    Open Teams Settings

    Click your profile picture → Settings → Devices.

  • 3

    Select Iriun under Camera

    In the Camera section, choose Iriun Webcam from the dropdown. A preview shows below.

Google Meet

Use Iriun Webcam in Google Meet

  • 1

    Start Iriun and connect phone

    Open the Iriun PC client and connect your phone before joining a Meet call.

  • 2

    Join or start a meeting

    In Google Meet (Chrome or Edge), click the three dots → Settings → Video.

  • 3

    Select Iriun Webcam

    In the Camera dropdown, choose Iriun Webcam. Allow camera access if Chrome prompts for permissions.

FAQ

Video call questions

Iriun Webcam not showing in Zoom camera list
Close Zoom completely, start the Iriun PC client, connect your phone, then reopen Zoom. The virtual camera must be registered before Zoom starts. If still missing, reboot Windows.
Teams shows black screen with Iriun selected
Check Windows camera permissions: Settings → Privacy → Camera → make sure Microsoft Teams is allowed. Also try: close Teams, reconnect Iriun phone, reopen Teams.
Google Meet says camera is in use by another app
Only one app can use the Iriun virtual camera at a time. Close any other app that might be using it (OBS, Skype, other browsers). Then refresh Google Meet.
