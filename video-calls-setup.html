Use Iriun Webcam with Zoom, Microsoft Teams & Google Meet

Select Iriun Webcam in your video app and go live in seconds. Use USB for the best latency; on Wi‑Fi use a 5GHz network for stability.

Install guide Reduce lag: USB vs Wi‑Fi
Version: 2.9.5 • OS: Windows 11 / 10 • File: IriunWebcam-2.9.5.exe

Before you start

  • Install Iriun on Windows and your phone
  • Open the phone app first, then the PC app
  • Connect by USB (best) or the same Wi‑Fi network
  • Allow Windows camera permissions

Zoom setup

Open settings → Zoom → Settings → Video.
Select camera → Choose Iriun Webcam. Enable HD if available.
Audio → Choose your preferred microphone (PC mic or external). Iriun handles video only.
If you don’t see it → Quit Zoom completely and reopen it after starting Iriun on your phone and PC.

Microsoft Teams setup

Open Devices → Teams → Settings → Devices.
Select camera → Under Camera choose Iriun Webcam.
Restart Teams → If missing, sign out or fully quit and relaunch after starting Iriun.
Privacy → Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → Allow apps access.

Google Meet setup

Open Meet settings → Start/join a call → Settings → Video.
Select camera → Choose Iriun Webcam and allow camera permission in the browser.
Refresh → Reload the tab after starting Iriun on phone and PC if the camera isn’t listed.
Audio → Select a mic in Meet settings; Iriun provides video only.

Improve quality & stability

  • Use USB for lowest latency — best for presentations and OBS scenes.
  • On Wi‑Fi — prefer 5GHz near the router; avoid heavy streaming/downloads.
  • Lighting — a small desk light improves image quality dramatically.
  • Phone power — keep your phone plugged in and disable battery savers.
  • Resolution — in apps, enable HD or 720p/1080p if your connection allows.
For streaming tips see OBS setup. If your camera is not detected, visit Troubleshooting.

FAQ (video calls)

  • Black screen? Restart the video app, then Iriun. Try USB or a better Wi‑Fi signal.
  • Choppy video? Close heavy apps, use 5GHz Wi‑Fi, or switch to USB.
  • Switch cameras? Use the phone app to switch front/rear camera.
  • Audio? Select your preferred microphone in the call app settings.
