How it works

Turn your phone into a webcam in 3 steps

  • 1

    Install on both devices

    Download IriunWebcam-2.9.5.exe on your Windows PC. Install the Iriun Webcam app on your iPhone or Android phone from the App Store or Google Play.

  • 2

    Connect via USB or Wi-Fi

    Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network, or plug your phone into the PC with a USB cable. Open the app on your phone — it connects automatically to the PC client.

  • 3

    Select Iriun in your app

    In Zoom, Teams, OBS or any app, go to the camera settings and choose Iriun Webcam as the camera source. Your phone camera is now streaming live.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is Iriun Webcam free?
Yes, the basic version is free. The free version shows a small watermark in the corner of the video. The paid version removes the watermark and adds higher resolution options. See the free vs paid comparison.
Does Iriun work with USB or only Wi-Fi?
Both. USB gives the lowest latency (1-5ms) and does not depend on your Wi-Fi quality. Wi-Fi is more convenient and works wirelessly. See the USB vs Wi-Fi guide.
Iriun not connecting — quick fix?
Make sure both your phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi network. The PC client must be running before you open the phone app. If still not connecting, try USB mode or check your firewall. See the not connecting fix guide.
