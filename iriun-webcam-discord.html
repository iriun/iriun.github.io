Discord guide

Iriun Webcam in Discord on Windows — setup & fix guide

Set Iriun Webcam as your camera in Discord on Windows. Open Voice & Video settings, select Iriun Webcam and fix camera not showing or black screen in Discord.

Iriun Webcam works with Discord as a standard virtual camera. The PC client must be running before you open Discord or start a video call.
Setup

Use Iriun Webcam in Discord

  • 1

    Start Iriun and connect phone

    Open the Iriun PC client on Windows. Connect your phone via USB or Wi-Fi. Confirm the camera preview is showing.

  • 2

    Open Discord Settings

    In Discord: click the gear icon (User Settings) at the bottom left → Voice & Video (under App Settings).

  • 3

    Select Iriun Webcam

    Scroll to the Video Settings section. In the Camera dropdown, select Iriun Webcam. A preview appears below.

  • 4

    Test in a call

    Start a video call in any server or DM. Click the camera icon to enable video — it uses Iriun Webcam automatically.

Common Discord issues

Fix Iriun not working in Discord

Iriun not showing in Discord camera dropdown
Discord was opened before Iriun connected
Close Discord, start Iriun + connect phone, reopen Discord
Discord shows black screen with Iriun selected
Camera permission denied in Windows or Discord
Settings > Privacy > Camera > allow Discord to use camera
Iriun not working on Discord after Windows update
Camera permissions reset by Windows update
Settings > Privacy & security > Camera > re-enable all access
FAQ

Discord questions

Iriun not working on Discord — camera spins/loads forever
Discord is having trouble initialising the virtual camera. Fix: close Discord completely (system tray), make sure Iriun PC client is running and phone is connected, then reopen Discord and try again. If persistent, reboot Windows.
Can I use Iriun for Discord screen share too?
Iriun provides the camera feed only — it does not affect Discord screen sharing. You can use both simultaneously: Iriun for your webcam video and Discord screen share for your screen.
