Iriun Webcam free vs paid — what is the difference?

Iriun Webcam free version: watermark and 720p max. Paid version: no watermark, 1080p. All other features including USB, Wi-Fi, microphone and all apps are free.

Quick answer

Free or paid — which should you get?

Start with free. The free version has all core features. Buy paid only if you need to remove the watermark for professional use or want 1080p resolution.

Free version limits

What the free version cannot do

  • Watermark — a small Iriun logo appears in the corner of the video. Visible to call participants and in recordings.
  • Maximum 720p — the free version caps at 720p resolution. Still sharper than many cheap USB webcams, but not 1080p.
The free version has no time limit and no feature restrictions beyond the two above. All connection modes, all apps, all platforms, microphone audio — all free.
How to buy

How to upgrade to the paid version

  • Open the Iriun app on your phone (iPhone or Android)
  • Tap the crown icon or Upgrade button in the app
  • Purchase via the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android)
  • The upgrade unlocks immediately on that phone — no code or PC change needed
FAQ

Free vs paid questions

Iriun webcam free version limitations — full list?
The free version has exactly two limitations: a small watermark overlay in the video, and a maximum resolution of 720p. Everything else — USB, Wi-Fi, all apps, microphone audio, iPhone and Android support — is available for free with no time limit.
How much does the Iriun paid version cost?
The paid upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase in the Iriun phone app. The price varies by region but is typically a few dollars. Check the App Store or Google Play for the current price in your country.
Does the paid upgrade work on both iPhone and Android?
The upgrade is per-platform — purchasing on iPhone unlocks it for that iPhone, and purchasing on Android unlocks it for that Android device. It is not a cross-platform purchase.
