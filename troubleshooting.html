Troubleshooting Iriun on Windows: Not Detected, Black Screen, Driver Issues

Follow this checklist to fix detection problems, black screens and driver issues on Windows 11/10. Prefer USB for the most stable connection.

Clean reinstall guide USB vs Wi‑Fi tips
Version: 2.9.5 • OS: Windows 11 / 10 • File: IriunWebcam-2.9.5.exe
Quick check: Open Iriun on your phone first, then the Windows app, then your video app. If on Wi‑Fi, ensure both devices are on the same network (prefer 5GHz).

Fast fixes

  • Restart the video app after starting Iriun
  • Check Windows camera privacy settings
  • Try a USB cable and different port
  • Reinstall Iriun if the device is missing
Last updated:
—

1) Checklist: not detected in apps

Start order: Phone app → Windows app → Video app (Zoom/Teams/OBS). Fully quit and reopen the video app.
Camera privacy: Windows → Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → Allow apps to access your camera.
USB for stability: Use a data‑capable USB cable. Try a different USB port.
Conflict check: Close other virtual camera tools (e.g., other webcam utilities) and background capture apps.
Reinstall (if needed): If Iriun Webcam device is missing in apps, see Clean reinstall.

2) Black screen or frozen image

  • Confirm the phone app is open and active; switch rear/front camera in the phone app.
  • On Wi‑Fi, connect both devices to the same SSID (prefer 5GHz). Move closer to the router.
  • Close heavy downloads/streams; reduce resolution to 720p in your video app.
  • Switch to USB for best stability.

3) USB connection problems

  • Use a proper data cable; avoid charge‑only cables. Try a different port (USB 3.x preferred).
  • Unlock phone; on iOS, tap "Trust this computer"; on Android, ensure data transfer is allowed.
  • Restart the Iriun Windows app after connecting the phone.
  • If still not detected, perform a clean reinstall.

4) Windows permissions & firewall

  • Privacy: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → Allow apps to access.
  • Firewall (Wi‑Fi only): Allow Iriun through Windows Defender Firewall on private networks.
  • Antivirus: Temporarily allow the installer/app if blocked; re‑enable after setup.

5) When to clean reinstall

If Iriun Webcam never appears as a camera device in any app or you suspect a corrupted install:

  • Uninstall Iriun from Windows
  • Remove the virtual camera in Device Manager
  • Reboot your PC
  • Install fresh and verify in Zoom/Teams/OBS
Follow the detailed steps in Uninstall & Clean Reinstall.
